# Instructions for This Repository

- This repository is the shared engineering baseline. Keep its requirements technology-neutral unless a document is explicitly stack-specific.
- All authored content must remain Markdown. Do not add secrets, customer data, credentials, generated binaries, or private internal details.
- Use MUST only for a deliberate baseline requirement; use SHOULD for a strong recommendation and MAY for an option. State scope, exception path, owner, and risk when a rule needs tailoring.
- For security, identity, privacy, accessibility, or operational claims, prefer current primary sources and link directly to the relevant source. Record versions and review dates where drift matters.
- Do not claim the handbook makes a project compliant or certified. Project owners must map requirements to their contracts, laws, and audit scope.
- Keep examples actionable and auditable. Avoid arbitrary numeric thresholds when they depend on data tier, business impact, or threat model.
- Update `CHANGELOG.md` when baseline requirements change, and review internal links after moving documents.
